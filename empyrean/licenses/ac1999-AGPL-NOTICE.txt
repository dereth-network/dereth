ac1999
https://gitlab.com/adamplumb/ac1999

Empyrean's Launch-era world base is converted, by `empyrean-import ac1999`, from ac1999's world
database and treasure tables, and Empyrean's source loot generator (the ac1999 loot rules) is
ported from ac1999's server, its treasure generator and random ranges. Both are the work of
ac1999's authors.

`empyrean-relay` speaks ac1999's protocol, and its readers and writers for that protocol are ported
from ac1999's server (its client events, chat, object and player messages, books, vendors,
fellowships, allegiances and appraisal), using facts about those messages stated on the message
pages of its knowledge base. Those ports were made and changed for Empyrean in 2026.
