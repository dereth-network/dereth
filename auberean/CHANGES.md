# Auberean changes

What each Auberean release brings for the people who play Asheron's Call 2 with it, a few lines each.

Add a line under **Unreleased** when a change lands that a player would notice. Say what they see,
not how it was done.

## Unreleased

- First pieces of the Asheron's Call 2 client: an offline world viewer and a headless client.
- The headless client can play on Yalain: fight, loot, talk and take portals.
- Viewer: shadows, drifting cloud shadows, water reflections, and the level-up, arrival and portal effects.
- A desktop window to walk the world in, with the game's default keys and chase camera.
- The game's own screens: pick or make a character, then play with the status bar, toolbar and chat.
- People, creatures and players wear their clothes and colors.
- Character creation shows its scenes, and the appearance sliders and Randomize work.
- Portal space when entering the world and on portal trips.
- Smoother movement for you, the camera and everyone around you.
- Steps and other things indoors are solid, and the camera no longer ends up inside them.
- Indoors is lit by its own lights instead of the outdoor sun.
- The screens before the world fill a larger window, and Credits plays.
- The game's own mouse cursors; buttons, lists, scroll bars and windows respond as in the original.
- Chat lines read as the game writes them, without a "[F]" after women's names.
- Gateways swirl, and torches, braziers and crystals burn and glow.
- Halls and dungeons wear their own floors and walls, like the start hall's grass.
- The shortcut bar shows your skills; use them with 1-0 or a click, and drag them around.
- Select things by clicking (held weapons too) or by key; names over heads; the radar shows what is around.
- Chat has its four windows, colored messages, slash commands, a typing history and its options.
- Opening pictures, a connecting screen, and Controls to see and change your keys.
- Creatures and objects deep inside halls and dungeons now show up.
- Drag an item out of your inventory to drop it on the ground.
- Jumping plays its animation: a crouch before a standing jump, then the leap.
- The options window's Client page lists the graphics settings.
- Polished metal and stone catch the light with a proper shine.
- People and creatures cast shadows, and torches light the ground around them at night.
- Fixed the starting guide book showing up twice in your pack.
- The map and social windows: the world map, closer maps, fellowship, allegiance and friends.
- In first person you now see your own body during combat.
- Effects can now shake the camera and flash the screen.
- The landscape draw distance and frill distance options now change how far the world is drawn.
- The sun, moon and clouds move across the sky smoothly instead of every few seconds.
- Trees, rocks and grass no longer grow inside buildings and ruins that keep their ground clear.
- Combat plays: attacks animate, hits show their effects and damage numbers, arrows fly from the drawn bow, and the dead stay down.
- Fixed other players' weapons staying in their hands after they put them away.
- Chat aliases: make one with /alias, then type ;name to use it.
- A toolbar slot holding a line of text says it when used.
- The inventory's paper doll shows your character, in front of the chat and radar windows.
- A new install starts with the original's recommended graphics settings.
- Fires burn in their own colors, like the camp's blue Arcane Fire.
- Mist parts around people walking through it.
- Flames, sparks and mist over water are no longer hidden by the water.
- The sun throws a lens flare, and cloud shadows and water ripples now move.
- /loc shows where you are, and ;loc says it in chat.
- New characters start with a random look.
- Server announcements show in chat.
- Halls open to the sky show it, and cave mouths show the land outside.
- Torchlight flickers indoors, and flowing or shimmering surfaces move.
- Wounded creatures look wounded until they heal.
- Your character now shows the clothes and armor you put on.
- You turn to face what you attack.
- Keys set in Controls now work in play and are kept between sessions.
- Logging in while your character is still in the world says so, instead of waiting forever.
- Alt+Enter switches between full screen and a window, and the choice is kept.
- Friends, presence, ignore, away and /tell Name@World work on worlds with a friends service.
- Crystals and other shiny surfaces show their reflections.
