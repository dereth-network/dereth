# Auberean changes

What each Auberean release brings for the people who play Asheron's Call 2 with it, a few lines each.

Add a line under **Unreleased** when a change lands that a player would notice. Say what they see,
not how it was done.

## Unreleased

- Comes with the Dereth Network launcher, which opens in Auberean's realm, keeps it up to date, and starts your retail client too, with friends.
- A desktop window to walk Asheron's Call 2's world in, with the game's default keys and chase camera.
- The game's own screens: opening pictures, a connecting screen, then pick or make a character and play.
- The screens before the world fill a larger window, and Credits plays.
- Character creation shows its scenes; new characters start with a random look, and the sliders and Randomize work.
- The status bar, toolbar and chat, with the game's own mouse cursors, buttons, lists, scroll bars and windows.
- Controls shows your keys and changes them, and your keys are kept between sessions.
- The options window's graphics settings start from the original's recommended ones.
- The landscape and frill draw distances change how far the world is drawn.
- Alt+Enter switches between full screen and a window, and the choice is kept.
- Logging in while your character is still in the world says so, instead of waiting forever.
- Messages sent as you enter the world show in the chat window.
- Smoother movement for you, the camera and everyone around you.
- Jumping plays its animation: a crouch before a standing jump, then the leap.
- You turn to face what you attack.
- People, creatures and players wear their clothes, armor and colors, your own character included.
- Wounded creatures look wounded until they heal.
- Portal space when entering the world and on portal trips.
- Steps and other things indoors are solid, and the camera stays out of them.
- Indoors is lit by its own lights, and torchlight flickers.
- Halls and dungeons wear their own floors and walls, with their creatures and objects deep inside.
- Halls open to the sky show it, and cave mouths show the land outside.
- Gateways swirl, and torches, braziers and crystals burn and glow.
- Fires burn in their own colors, and flames, sparks and mist show over water.
- Mist parts around people walking through it, and flowing or shimmering surfaces move.
- Polished metal and stone catch the light with a proper shine.
- People and creatures cast shadows, and torches and spells light the people and things around them.
- The sun, moon and clouds move smoothly, and the sun throws a lens flare.
- Cloud shadows and water ripples move.
- Trees, rocks and grass stay out of buildings and ruins that keep their ground clear.
- Effects can shake the camera and flash the screen.
- The shortcut bar shows your skills; use them with 1-0 or a click, and drag them around.
- A shortcut bar slot holding a line of text says it when used.
- Select things by clicking (held weapons too) or by key; names over heads; the radar shows what is around.
- Chat has its four windows, colored messages, slash commands, a typing history and its options.
- Chat lines read as the game writes them, and server announcements show there.
- /loc shows where you are, and ;loc says it in chat.
- Chat aliases: make one with /alias, then type ;name to use it.
- The inventory's paper doll shows your character, in front of the chat and radar windows.
- Drag an item out of your inventory to drop it on the ground.
- The map and social windows: the world map, closer maps, fellowship, allegiance and friends.
- Combat plays: attacks animate, hits show their effects and damage numbers, and the dead stay down.
- Arrows fly from the drawn bow, and in first person you see your own body in combat.
- Friends, presence, ignore, away and /tell Name@World work on worlds with a friends service.
- Crystals and other shiny surfaces show their reflections.
