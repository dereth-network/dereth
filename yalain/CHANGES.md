# Yalain changes

What each Yalain release brings for server operators and players, a few lines each.

Add a line under **Unreleased** when a change lands that an operator or a player would notice. Say
what they see, not how it was done.

## Unreleased
- Spellbinding an item with nothing it can take now says why.
- Transmute All now waits for your pick-up animation, unless you jump or attack.
- Examining a hero now shows its hero title.
- Examining signs, doors, lifestones and shrines now shows their names and descriptions.
- Recalls now take as long as each race's own animation.
- Consignments are now limited at each broker: three a player, seven an account.
- Totems now ask five levels below their source, and totems and craft raises say why they're refused.
- Items on your trade offer can't be used, and closing a corpse stops a Take All.
- Moving, attacking or dying now interrupts a craft, which then makes and costs nothing.
- Equipping is refused while riding or swimming, and a robe won't replace two pieces at once.
- World: heroes regain focus while logged out.
- World: lifestones stand where the wiki and players' maps of the time show them.
- World: any creature can leave a lodestone or a Tessera, by your level.
- World: joining a kingdom needs level 10, and the war and peace shrines work.
- World: monarchs can bind their allegiance's recall at the halls' binding stones.
- World: treasure above level 50 hits and protects as retail's did; enchanted gear is worth more.
- World: keyed dungeon caches hold treasure, and now and then a rare.
- Fixed level-ups ignoring your buffs and vitae, and showing too few skill credits.
- Fewer monsters play a victory emote, and they finish it before calming down.
- Buffs and heals on other players follow the kingdom rules, and pets can fight players.
- Fixed helpful skills used only on others, like Ease Memories, being refused outside PvP.
- Worlds can now give back a random amount of vitae.
- Master of Doctrine's bonus now raises your maximum vigor itself, with no lasting effect.
- Monarchs can appoint eight officers, and a kingdom restriction keeps members in its kingdom.
- Leveling up or using a Tessera while untraining a skill no longer hides the untraining.
- Fixed flying creatures' corpses staying in the air.
- Using a teaching stone at a vault or elsewhere now shows its page.
- Worlds can now give examine text to objects whose template has none.
- Pets follow you to a new target, and a creature's kin join its fight with a pet.
- Selected players and fellows now show the damage other players did them.
- Worlds can now have an effect put on one of a list of effects at random.
- Fellowship dismissals, a full fellowship and broken allegiances now say so.
- Death Hardiness, Anatomy, Prospector and the miners' perks now work.
- A camp's period now runs from when the dead creature's body leaves, as in retail.
- Fixed the admin teleport to map coordinates landing in the wrong place.
- Effects that raise or lower the heals you receive now work.
- Outdoors, players and creatures stay in view, and in earshot, as far as the next landblock.
- Moving while walking to a recruit now cancels the recruitment.
