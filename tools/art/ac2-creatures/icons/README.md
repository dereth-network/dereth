# New Dereth's creature icons

The icons here are original art, drawn for Dereth Network, and are under this repository's MIT
license.

Each family's icon is `<family>.png` (its creature table's `name`): a 32 × 32 picture, RGB or RGBA,
8 bits a channel. The land converter writes it as a picture of the family's own and gives the
family's weenies that icon. A family without one here keeps the placeholder its table names.
